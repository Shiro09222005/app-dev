# 🎬 Favorite Series: Jujutsu Kaisen

**Jujutsu Kaisen** (*Sorcery Fight*) is a Japanese dark fantasy anime series based on the manga written and illustrated by Gege Akutami.

> *"Stand proud. You are strong."* — Sukuna

---

## 📖 Synopsis
The story follows high school student **Yuji Itadori** as he joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers to eliminate a powerful Curse named **Ryomen Sukuna**, of whom Yuji becomes the host.

## ⭐ Why I Recommend It
* **Top-tier Animation:** Produced by studio *MAPPA* with fluid, high-impact fight choreography.
* **Compelling Power System:** Combines *Cursed Energy*, *Innate Techniques*, and *Domain Expansions*.
* **Memorable Characters:** Features well-written mentors and antagonists rather than generic tropes.

## 👥 Top 3 Characters
1. **Satoru Gojo** – The strongest Jujutsu Sorcerer and teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High.
2. **Toji Fushiguro** – The "Sorcerer Killer" who relies purely on physical prowess via *Heavenly Restriction*.
3. **Megumi Fushiguro** – A pragmatic first-year student wielding the *Ten Shadows Technique*.
